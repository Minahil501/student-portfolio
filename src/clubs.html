Club Categories

UCP has a club for almost every interest. Browse the categories below and find the one that fits you best.

💻

Technology Clubs

For students who love coding, robotics, AI and building things. Members work on projects, attend hackathons and run hands on workshops.

  • Tech Society
  • Robotics Club
  • Coding Circle
🏏

Sports Clubs

Stay active and represent UCP in inter university tournaments. Regular practice sessions are open to beginners and experienced players.

  • Cricket
  • Futsal
  • Badminton
🎭

Cultural Clubs

Celebrate music, drama, art and traditions. Cultural clubs organize performances, exhibitions and festivals throughout the year.

  • Music Club
  • Dramatics
  • Arts and Crafts
🎤

Debate Societies

Sharpen your public speaking and critical thinking. Debaters take part in parliamentary debates, MUNs and declamation contests.

  • Debating Society
  • MUN Club
  • Literary Circle
🌱

Community Service Clubs

Give back to society through donation drives, tree plantation, tutoring programs and awareness campaigns.

  • Welfare Society
  • Green Campus
  • Blood Donor Circle
📸

Media Clubs

For photographers, filmmakers and content creators who capture campus life and cover university events.

  • Photography Club
  • Film Society
  • Content Creators

Can't find your interest?

Any group of students can propose a new club to the Student Affairs office with a faculty advisor and at least 15 members.
