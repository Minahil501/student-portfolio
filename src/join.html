How to Join a Club

Joining a club at UCP is quick and free. Follow these four simple steps to get started.

  1. Explore

    Browse the club categories and pick one or two clubs that match your interests.

  2. Register

    Fill in the club registration form at the Clubs Fair or online using your student ID.

  3. Attend orientation

    Join the welcome session to meet the club team and learn about upcoming plans.

  4. Get involved

    Attend meetings, volunteer at events and apply for team roles as you grow.

Participation guidelines

  • Carry your UCP student ID card to all club activities.
  • Attend at least 70% of club meetings to keep active membership.
  • Respect all members and follow the university code of conduct.
  • Inform your club team in advance if you cannot attend an event.
  • Club activities must not clash with your scheduled classes.
  • Use club resources and equipment responsibly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I join more than one club?

Yes. You can join up to three clubs, as long as you can attend their meetings.

Is there a membership fee?

No. Joining a club is free. Some trips or competitions may have a small participation fee.

Can first semester students join?

Absolutely. Every club welcomes new students, and no experience is needed.
